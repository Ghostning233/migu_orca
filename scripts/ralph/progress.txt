## Codebase Patterns
- Flask `/api/v1` routes use `<path:param>` while OpenAPI uses `{param}`; normalize both forms when checking contract drift.
- `docs/api/openapi.json` is deterministic output from `apps.migu_orca_web.openapi_gen`; regenerate it after changing contract metadata.
- `TrackingRecord` is the external submission authority; legacy queue and AB result data are compatibility projections or migration evidence.
- Public tracking action/status DTOs use finite enums, while request-scoped credentials/capability observations stay out of durable or response contracts.
- Frontend contract fixtures should derive request/response shapes from `frontend/src/api/generated/api.ts`; use type-only assertions instead of feature-local DTO interfaces.
